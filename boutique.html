Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No — walk-ins are always welcome during our hours (Thursday–Sunday, 10am–6pm). If you'd like a specific time reserved, just reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

How far in advance should I book?

Since walk-ins are welcome anytime we're open, most customers don't need to book ahead. If you'd like a reserved appointment, let us know your preferred date.

How much do dresses cost?

Quinceañera gowns start at $680 for the dress alone (packages with everything included start at $1,500), and bridesmaid dresses range from $70–$165. Ask us in person about pricing for bridal and formal wear.

Is there a deposit required?

Reserving a dress requires a $200 deposit. We also offer a weekly payment plan — as low as $100 a week over about three months.

Do you offer alterations?

We're confirming our alterations policy — ask us in store for the latest, and check back here soon.

What if I damage a dress while trying it on?

Please be careful with makeup and deodorant, which can stain, and take care not to tear the fabric. If a garment is damaged while in your care, you'll be responsible for the cost of the damage.

What if there's a problem with my purchase?

If a Liz Fashion product is defective, we'll issue a refund or send a replacement — but you must report it within 48 hours of purchase and have your receipt. We're unable to issue refunds after that window or without a receipt.

What should I bring when I visit?

Comfortable undergarments similar to what you'd wear on the day, and — if you have them — the shoes and any accessories you're planning to pair with your gown.
