Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — every fitting is private and appointment-based so your stylist can give you their full attention. Use the form below or call ahead to reserve a time.

How far in advance should I book?

For bridal and quinceañera gowns that need ordering and alterations, we recommend starting several months out. For formal and prom, we can often accommodate shorter timelines — ask us about your date.
